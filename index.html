Everyday help

Useful when life is full.

Naza is built for the moments when you want a second brain, a bit of encouragement, or a simple answer from the things already around you.

✦

Talk it through

Ask a question, untangle a plan, write something, or just put a thought into words. Naza meets you where you are.

⌂

Make meals from home

Take a picture of your fridge and get gentle ideas for what to use, what may need attention, and what to cook next.

◌

Cook with confidence

Get a helpful read on what you can see while food bakes, with reminders to use your own judgment and kitchen basics.

A private space

Your life is not a product.

Naza is designed to live on your device. Your conversations, notes, and kitchen check-ins are for you—not for an advertising company or a feed.

  • No account needed to get started
  • No ads following you around
  • Your personal information stays under your control
  • A lock screen is there when you want one
⌁

A space that’s yours

Some things are meant to stay personal. Naza keeps the focus on helping you—not collecting you.
